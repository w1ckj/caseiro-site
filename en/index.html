Invoices

Scan the QR code. Everything is kept.

Every Portuguese invoice has a QR code, and it holds far more than the amount. Your helper reads it and keeps all of it:

  • Issuer’s tax number
  • Buyer’s tax number
  • Type and number
  • ATCUD
  • Date
  • Base and VAT by rate
  • Total
  • Withholding tax
  • Control code
  • The code itself stays with you. Show it large to whoever needs it and it scans just like the one on the invoice.
  • It knows who issued it. It looks up the name by tax number in the EU VAT register.
  • No duplicates. You’re warned if the invoice is already recorded, and a credit note goes in as a refund.

Always tells you where it came from

No number appears without its source. If it doesn’t know, it says so.

  • Mortgage rate6-month Euribor for March, published by the European Central Bank
  • Electricity billRead from the QR code; supplier name from the tax number, in the EU VAT register
  • Monthly paymentWorked out by the simulator; check it against your bank statement
